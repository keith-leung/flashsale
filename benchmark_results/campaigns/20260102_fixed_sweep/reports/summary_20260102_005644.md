### Adaptive Plateau Detection

1. **Start Small:** Begin with low concurrency (t=4, c=10)
2. **Analyze Growth:** Calculate throughput increase percentage
3. **Adaptive Adjustment:**

   - **Moderate Growth (2-5%):** Increase moderately (threads × 1.2, concurrency × 1.5)

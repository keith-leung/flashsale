## Methodology

### Adaptive Plateau Detection

Tests use intelligent adaptive testing to find true performance plateaus:

1. **Start Small:** Begin with low concurrency (t=4, c=10)
2. **Analyze Growth:** Calculate throughput increase percentage
3. **Adaptive Adjustment:**
   - **Significant Growth (>5%):** Increase aggressively (threads × 1.5, concurrency × 2)
   - **Moderate Growth (2-5%):** Increase moderately (threads × 1.2, concurrency × 1.5)
   - **Marginal Growth (0-2%):** Increase slightly to confirm plateau (threads × 1.1, concurrency × 1.2)
4. **Plateau Detection:** <2% variance across 3 consecutive tests → PLATEAU CONFIRMED
5. **Error Policy:** Stop immediately on ANY 503 error → SYSTEM_LIMIT
6. **Caps:** Maximum threads=24, concurrency=2000

### Decision Types

- **✓ PLATEAU_CONFIRMED:** True performance plateau detected (<2% variance)
- **🔝 MAX_CAPS_REACHED:** Hit maximum thread/concurrency caps
- **🛑 SYSTEM_LIMIT:** Errors detected (503s), safe limit found
- **⚠ INCOMPLETE:** Testing stopped before plateau detection
